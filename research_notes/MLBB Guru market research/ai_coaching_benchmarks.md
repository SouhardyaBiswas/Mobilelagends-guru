## 1. PC MOBA companion apps: offer, pricing, users, funding, revenue, Overwolf economics

### Takeaway
PC MOBA companion apps use the same playbook: free stats, builds and overlays paid for mainly by ads, plus a cheap optional subscription ($3–5/month, usually ad removal and some extra features). The scale ones grew to millions of users, but third-party revenue estimates are only in the low tens of millions of dollars. Exits have come through acquisition (Mobalytics to ESL FACEIT Group in 2025), not stand-alone breakout revenue. Overwolf, the platform underneath many of them, paid creators $300M+ in 2025, which shows the ad and subscription model does pay at scale.

### Cited Findings
**Mobalytics**
- Seed round of $2.6M from Almaz Capital, Founders Fund, General Catalyst and GGV Capital **[pre-2025, 2016]** — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2016/11/16/gaming-analytics-startup-mobalytics-grabs-2-6-million-in-seed-funding)
- Total funding estimates conflict: $13.8M over 5 rounds from 14 investors (Tracxn), $14.0M (Startupintros), $16.8M (PitchBook). A 2020 round is often cited at ~$11.25M — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/mobalytics/__LuEwOUQusuEtwTUvqZyCoGtRJ23eHBU1_jkf12aXx9s); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/166360-06); [Startupintros](https://startupintros.com/orgs/mobalytics)
- ESL FACEIT Group acquired Mobalytics in March 2025 (dated 17 Mar 2025 by PitchBook and Tracxn; 18 Mar by CB Insights). Financial terms were not disclosed. Mobalytics was to run as a standalone business while EFG looked for ways to integrate it — [Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2025/03/esl-faceit-group-mobalytics-acquisition); [TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/news/mobalytics-acquired-by-esl-faceit-group-to-enhance-esports-analytics/)
- Users: "over 10 million users worldwide" (Datanyze); "more than 7 million players" in 2020 **[pre-2025]** — [Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/mobalytics/444184774); [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Mobalytics)
- Revenue **[estimate]**: $3.8M (Datanyze), ~$9M/yr (Growjo), $5–25M (Owler). PitchBook leaves revenue blank — [Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/mobalytics/444184774); [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Mobalytics); [Owler](https://www.owler.com/company/mobalytics)
- Model: freemium with a premium subscription for advanced tools, plus partnerships and sponsorships — [TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/news/mobalytics-acquired-by-esl-faceit-group-to-enhance-esports-analytics/)

**Blitz.gg**
- Google Ads publisher case study (undated): grew to 3 million daily active users in 3 years and was "well on their way to hitting 10M monthly visitors". Revenue "significantly grown with the introduction of digital ads on our site". It also mentions that TSM's parent Swift Media helped acquire and run the Blitz App — [Google Ads publisher story](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/blitz/)
- Another profile claims "8 million+ active users" (metric undefined) — [LeadIQ](https://leadiq.com/c/blitz/5e471892626d3b6fe08fcfba)
- Funding: $3.8M over 4 rounds (YC, BoxGroup, Greylock) **[unverified]** — [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/c/Blitz-App)
- Premium price: $4.99/month standard and "may vary depending on your region", from Blitz's own support page (about 2.5 years old) — [Blitz Support](https://support.blitz.gg/hc/en-us/articles/4415422406425-How-Much-Does-Blitz-Premium-Cost). A 2026 comparison lists $3.99/month or $32.04/year (~$2.67/month) — [Hexgate, vendor](https://hexgate.app/blog/blitz-vs-u-gg/)

**U.GG**
- U.GG Plus removes ads for $3.99/month or $29.88/year, with a 7-day free trial on annual reported — [Hexgate, vendor](https://hexgate.app/blog/blitz-vs-u-gg/)

**OP.GG**
- Its own media kit claims "300M+ gamers a year" across 23 products — [OP.GG advertising](https://revenue.our.gg/advertise)
- A secondary profile gives ~55M monthly visitors and ~460M pageviews/month. Crunchbase says 28M+ monthly users (probably dated) — [yespress](https://yespress.io/op-gg); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/op-gg)
- Revenue **[estimate]**: $16.8M (ZoomInfo), $17.2M (ContactOut), $15–25M (Similarweb). Outliers: $8.2M (Hypestat) and $52M (Worth of Web). **Porofessor**
- Free with a premium subscription. No reliable official price was found. One low-authority blog says ~$5/month — [Hexgate, vendor](https://hexgate.app/blog/porofessor-vs-blitz/); [Boosting Market](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/best-lol-free-coaching-tools/)

**Dota Plus (Valve, first-party paid coaching)**
- $3.99/month, with discounts for 6- and 12-month commitments (reported as ~6% and ~12%). Can be bought in non-renewing time blocks — [Hawk.live guide](https://hawk.live/posts/dota-2-plus-guide); [skin.land 2026 review](https://skin.land/blog/is-dota-plus-worth-it-2026/)

**Overwolf ecosystem economics**
- Nov 2025: over $300M paid to in-game creators in 2025 and $800M cumulative. 178,000 creators, 113M MAU, 1,500+ supported games, $150M+ raised. The stated goal is $1B in annual creator payouts by 2030. Premium-mod authors earn 50% of revenue — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/overwolf-paid-out-300m-to-creators-so-far-this-year/); [Overwolf blog](https://blog.overwolf.com/developers-november-newsletter-300m-paid-to-in-game-creators/)
- 2025 was reported as +25% over 2024. 2024 is cited at ~$240M and 2023 at ~$201M (secondary) — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/overwolf-creator-payouts-hit-300-million/); [yespress](https://yespress.io/overwolf)
- Wikipedia claims more than $400M paid in 2026 (+33%). No corroborating source was found — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overwolf)
- Overwolf takes 20–30% of developer ad and subscription earnings — [Overwolf: Our commitment](https://www.overwolf.com/our-commitment/); [Overwolf Medium](https://medium.com/overwolf/how-does-overwolf-make-money-f70a195a4ea9)
- From 1 Oct 2026, the platform fee on Tebex-powered subscriptions drops to 5% (from the standard 30%). The stated aim is to get developers to offer subscriptions alongside ads — [Overwolf dev docs (Tebex)](https://dev.overwolf.com/ow-electron/monetization/subscriptions/implementation/)
- Overwolf recommends a freemium design with limited free uses per month, about three tiers, and a yearly plan at a 20–30% discount — [Overwolf subscriptions guide](https://dev.overwolf.com/ow-native/guides/growth/subscriptions/)

### Inferences
- The going price for a MOBA companion subscription is $3–5/month in Western markets, and annual plans bring it to ~$2.50–3.30/month. Even Valve's first-party coaching (Dota Plus) is priced at $3.99. Nobody in this category charges $10+/month for stats and builds.
- Advertising seems to be the main revenue line: Blitz credits ads for its revenue growth, OP.GG sells itself to advertisers, and U.GG's sub mainly removes ads. Overwolf cutting its subscription fee to 5% in 2026 suggests subscriptions had been under-adopted.
- Apps with 3M+ DAU appear to sit around ~$5–25M in revenue (estimates only). Revenue per user in this category is low.
- Mobalytics shows the realistic exit: a strategic acquirer (an esports or platform group) buys the user base. No large standalone IPO-style outcome was found.

### Gaps
- No official subscriber counts or free-to-paid conversion rates for Mobalytics, Blitz, U.GG, OP.GG or Porofessor. No official current Mobalytics Plus price (pricing page not retrieved).
- Facecheck and other smaller LoL/Dota tools were not researched for lack of tool budget. No data was found on Dota Plus subscriber numbers or revenue.
- Mobalytics' acquisition price is undisclosed.

## 2. AI coaching products: who uses live screen reading vs post-game replay analysis

### Takeaway
"AI coach" products split into two groups. Platform giants (Microsoft, NVIDIA, Razer, and Tencent inside its own game) ship free live assistants that read the screen or game state. Startups (Trophi.ai, Omnic.AI, Leetify, GOSU.AI) mostly charge a subscription for post-session telemetry or replay analysis. Live voice advice on a second screen, which is MLBB Guru's core interaction, is now a free feature of Microsoft's Xbox mobile app.

### Cited Findings
**Platform and hardware companies (live, free or bundled)**
- Microsoft Gaming Copilot (beta): rolled out to players aged 18+ in the PC Game Bar, and to the Xbox mobile app (iOS and Android) in October 2025. Available in all regions except mainland China — [TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/341140/gaming-copilot-beta-is-coming-to-windows-pc-and-xbox-on-mobile)
- Xbox Wire (28 May 2025) presented the mobile beta as a way to "access Copilot for Gaming on a second screen, without distracting you from your core gameplay experience" — [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/05/28/copilot-gaming-test-xbox-android-ios/)
- Reviewers called the mobile version a solid second screen for tips and basic build advice, and raised privacy concerns about PC screenshot analysis. Secondary sources say screenshot analysis is opt-in and PC-only, which was not confirmed with Microsoft — [FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/i-put-xboxs-gaming-copilot-through-hell-why); [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/threads/copilot-for-gaming-vs-project-g-assist-cloud-ai-vs-local-gpu-ai-for-windows-pc.385106/)
- NVIDIA G-Assist: launched in the NVIDIA app in March 2025 and runs locally on GeForce RTX GPUs (reported 8B-parameter local model; VRAM requirement first reported as 12GB, later 6GB). It mainly reads system and game state for settings and optimization. One review found it invented game items and strategies — [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2025/03/27/nvidia-app-gets-new-g-assist-gaming-ai-assistant-that-runs-locally-on-geforce-rtx-ai-pcs-xcxwbn/); [EAM Vision comparison](https://eamvisiondirect.com/ai-gaming-assistants-face-off-microsoft-copilot-vs-20251017/)
- Razer AI Game Copilot (formerly Project Ava): a voice coach that gives real-time tips (builds, boss fights) from game data gathered during play. It was in development with a beta sign-up when covered — [Laptop Mag](https://www.laptopmag.com/laptops/gaming-laptops-pcs/razer-ai-gaming-coach-copilot-microsoft-nvidia); [The Outpost](https://theoutpost.ai/news-story/razer-unveils-ai-powered-game-development-tools-qa-copilot-and-gamer-copilot-13454/)

**Startups (mostly post-session or replay, paid)**
- Trophi.ai (St. John's, Canada): $3.3M seed led by Build Ventures with Concrete Ventures and angels, for AI coaching in gaming and sim racing — [BeyondGames.biz](https://www.beyondgames.biz/36262/st-johns-ai-esports-coaching-startup-trophi-ai-secures-3-3m-seed-funding/); [trophi.ai press release](https://www.trophi.ai/press-release/trophi-ai-seed-funding)
- Trophi.ai pricing (2026): Premium $12.99/month, Premium + Setups $19.99, Team $29.99, Professional $69.99. Annual plans work out to ~$7.50/month. There is a 7-day trial but no permanent free plan — [Trophi pricing](https://www.trophi.ai/sim-racing/pricing/); [Sim Racing Manual](https://simracingmanual.com/resources/ai-driving-coaches/)
- A competitor's blog estimates Trophi.ai at ~$1.8M ARR **[unverified, competitor source]** — [SimCoaches](https://simcoaches.com/blogs/best-hydraulic-sim-racing-pedals-2025/trackpro-vs-trophi-ai-which-ai-coach-is-right-for-you)
- Omnic.AI: founded 2021. Its "Forge" tool analyzes player performance in Valorant, Fortnite, Rocket League and Overwatch 2. It was still active as of a March 2025 partnership with esports organization M80, and partnered with Belong Gaming in 2023 — [Yahoo Finance / M80 press release](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/m80-expands-access-ai-esports-162500706.html); [Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2023/09/omnic-ai-belong-gaming-francis-thai)
- Leetify (Sweden, CS): €900k seed; match analysis to find weaknesses; 10,000+ monthly users in beta **[pre-2025, 2020]** — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2020/05/13/leetify-seed-funding/)
- GOSU.AI: a LoL in-game voice assistant/coach. Raised $1.9M (Runa Capital, Mar 2018) and announced $2.8M (Dec 2019). Acquired by Sber in 2021 **[pre-2025]** — [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2019/12/10/gosu-ai-raises-2-8-million-ai-based-coaching-gamers-league-legends/); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/gosu_ai); [Runa Capital](https://runacap.com/companies/gosu/)
- Dojo Madness (Berlin): $4.5M Series A for a mobile app acting as a LoL esports coach **[pre-2025; date not in results]** — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1323146)

### Inferences
- MLBB Guru's interaction design (screenshot in, voice advice out, phone as second screen) has been validated by Microsoft. That also means it will be expected to be free: Microsoft, NVIDIA and Tencent bundle live assistants at no charge.
- Startups that charge real subscription prices (Trophi at $12.99+) sell measurable skill improvement to enthusiasts, in sim racing where telemetry APIs are rich. They do not sell live tips to casual new players.
- The hallucination complaints about G-Assist show the risk for any LLM coach that generates item and strategy advice without grounding in current patch data.

### Gaps
- No data was found on Insights.gg, GameLeap, iTero, Logitech or Asus AI assistants, or highlight tools (Moments, etc.): names were searched without useful results.
- No LLM-based AI-coach startup funding rounds from 2025–2026 turned up in search. That absence is itself a weak signal of limited VC appetite, but could also be a search limitation.
- Microsoft Gaming Copilot usage numbers were not found.

## 3. Mobile MOBA analogues (Wild Rift, Honor of Kings, Pokémon Unite, AoV, MLBB) and first-party AI

### Takeaway
In Honor of Kings, Tencent built the in-match AI coach itself. "Lingbao" is a generative-AI companion that gives live tactical alerts and reportedly voice-triggered item purchases, and it drew 10M+ participants on its first day in 2025. It is free and monetized through cosmetic skins. MLBB already has several indie AI-coach apps (Coach Legend, Mythic Coach, ModeCoach, mlbb.tools) with freemium, credit and ad models. Wild Rift's third-party scene is limited to stats and build apps plus Riot's official Riot Mobile app.

### Cited Findings
**Honor of Kings (Tencent)**
- Lingbao (灵宝) first appeared on the HoK homepage in early 2024 and gained voice interaction during matches in a later update. A June 2025 report headline claims over 10 million users participated on the first day — [93913.com](https://www.93913.com/112107.html)
- Lingbao analyzes live match data and gives tactical alerts, for example an ambush warning when an enemy hero disappears, or attack/retreat suggestions in team fights based on skill cooldowns — [93913.com](https://www.93913.com/112107.html)
- Monetization: paid skins for Lingbao, with some limited skins sold through blind-box draws costing several hundred to over 1,000 yuan — [93913.com](https://www.93913.com/112107.html)
- Backlash: in an April update Tencent added three interaction modes and an off switch for proactive chat, and softened a "sharp-tongued" persona. Players on Xiaohongshu complained it was "too noisy" — [93913.com](https://www.93913.com/112107.html); [Sina Finance](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/7264514723/1b0ffb2a300102hx2c); [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250529A07MDI00)
- A Sina headline: "王者荣耀灵宝功能引争议，说一声就能买装备，手速慢也能迅速出装" ("Lingbao feature sparks controversy: say one word and it buys items; even slow players can build fast"). The article has since been removed, so only the headline is verified — [Sina (removed)](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7857201856_1d45362c001907doho.html)
- Tencent SPARK 2026: Lingbao got a "full upgrade" to always-on companionship in and out of matches, and AI esports commentary launched. A summer update added the large-model AI teammate "Xiaotian". Sources conflict on whether Xiaotian is in HoK or Peacekeeper Elite — [Yomiqo](https://www.yomiqo.com/en/tencent-spark-2026-summary/); [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3827658881323649)
- HoK "AI Commander Mode": players give voice commands to AI teammates. It runs on Tencent's GVoice speech recognition, per Tencent Cloud promotional material — [Tencent Cloud](https://www.facebook.com/TencentCloud/videos/how-is-ai-redefining-moba-strategy-in-honor-of-kings-the-ai-commander-mode-lets-/941326038236569/)
- Lingbao is also in 王者万象棋 (HoK auto-chess) as a real-time voice coach for lineup suggestions, auction timing and tempo (July 2026 coverage) — [Gcores](https://www.gcores.com/articles/220106); [18183](https://www.18183.com/gonglue/202607/kcitbd23.html)
- Tencent's earlier research AI "Wukong" beat pro HoK teams. Tencent said it would use it for pro-player analysis, but no evidence was found of it shipping to players **[pre-2025]** — [Digital News Asia](https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/digital-economy/tencents-wukong-ai-solution-crushes-professional-honour-kings-players)

**Wild Rift (Riot)**
- Riot Mobile is the official companion app and supports LoL, VALORANT, Wild Rift, TFT and LoR — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/riot-mobile/id1077150310)
- Third-party Wild Rift apps are stats, tier and build tools: "Wild Rift Companion" (unofficial; win, ban and pick rates by rank), WR-META, and Probuilds — [Google Play: Wild Rift Companion](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wildriftcompanion.app); [Google Play: WR-META](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wmr&hl=en); [Google Play: Probuilds](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wuochoang.lolegacy)

**MLBB direct competitors (most relevant to MLBB Guru)**
- Coach Legend: MLBB AI Guide (Kanto Projects; iOS and Android). Features: Draft Coach (top-10 counters ranked by enemies beaten, win rate and synergy, plus an AI draft breakdown), hero pages with builds, emblems and battle spells, a daily live tier list of 130+ heroes, a Team Synergy score, Hero Finder, and "Coach Chat" — [Coach Legend site](https://coachlegend.kantoprojects.com/); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kantoprojects.draftlegend&hl=en)
- Coach Legend's monetization: all data is free and AI answers cost 1 credit each. New installs get 10 credits with no card. Each rewarded ad earns 10 credits, up to 10 ads a day, and credit packs are sold in-app (prices not shown). There is no subscription. Its stats come from "the last seven days of ranked matches via a public community MLBB data service". Footer: "not affiliated with or endorsed by Moonton" — [Coach Legend site](https://coachlegend.kantoprojects.com/)
- Other MLBB tools: Mythic Coach: MLBB AI Guide (Google Play), ModeCoach, mlbb.tools (counter-picks, synergy scores, ban suggestions), and the MLBBHub draft simulator. All say they are fan-made and unaffiliated — [Mythic Coach](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akrh.mlb_guide&hl=en); [ModeCoach](https://modecoach.live/); [mlbb.tools](https://mlbb.tools/); [MLBBHub](https://mlbbhub.com/draft-simulator)

### Inferences
- The HoK precedent is directly relevant: a publisher can ship "buy this next" and tactical voice prompts inside the client, free, with perfect game-state access and no screenshot latency, and pay for it with cosmetics. If Moonton copies Lingbao, third-party in-match advice for MLBB would lose most of its value. Draft and pre-game education tools would be less exposed.
- MLBB Guru's draft and pre-game features (counters, emblems, spells) are already commoditized by free or credit-based indie apps. Its differentiation is the screenshot-driven in-match advice and the voice second screen.
- Coach Legend's model (free data, scarce AI credits refilled by rewarded ads, optional packs) is designed around LLM inference costs and low willingness to pay. It is the closest working template for an MLBB AI product.

### Gaps
- No data on downloads or revenue for Coach Legend, Mythic Coach or ModeCoach. Pulling Play Store install counts would be a quick follow-up.
- No primary Moonton policy on companion or advisory apps (screen-reading or draft assistants) was found. A secondary source says Moonton is firmly against third-party apps, but that concerns scripts and cheats — [NEMA guide (secondary)](https://erp.nema.gov.mn/today-chronicle/mobile-legends-and-third-party-apps-what-you-need-to-know-1764814841)
- Pokémon Unite and Arena of Valor tools, and any official Moonton AI coach plans, were not researched or found.
- No confirmed Tencent statement on whether Lingbao's voice item-purchase feature is still live, or how it is restricted in ranked play.

## 4. Publisher policy changes and lessons for dependence (Riot / Vanguard)

### Takeaway
Riot has tightened rules on third-party LoL apps repeatedly since 2024: Vanguard blocked memory reading, enemy ult and cooldown trackers were removed in 2025, overlay ads were banned in 2025, OP.GG's spell tracker was pulled, and in Oct 2026 memory access was blocked by default with a grace period to April 2027. Each change removed live in-match features or ad inventory from the incumbent apps. Riot's stated reason in 2026 is AI-assisted cheating.

### Cited Findings
- April 2024 (Vanguard launch): Riot's FAQ said external tools reading game memory would stop working, and that there is no allow list — [Riot Vanguard FAQ](https://www.riotgames.com/en/DevRel/vanguard-faq) (via [Hexgate](https://hexgate.app/blog/riot-third-party-apps/))
- 13 March 2025: enemy ultimate timers were removed from apps. Enemy cooldown tracking and power-spike notifications were later removed too, so both Porofessor and Blitz lost the same features — [porofessor.org guide, vendor-adjacent](https://porofessor.org/guides/porofessor-vs-blitz/). Riot reportedly bans tracking of enemy ultimate cooldowns in third-party apps, whether automatic or manually entered — [Hexgate, vendor](https://hexgate.app/blog/can-you-get-banned-for-lol-apps/)
- 29 May 2025: Riot banned third-party ads in overlays, loading screens and the Riot Client — [porofessor.org](https://porofessor.org/guides/porofessor-vs-blitz/). Overwolf published a response defending creator monetization in June 2025 — [Overwolf blog](https://blog.overwolf.com/supporting-creators-in-riots-ecosystem-our-response-and-commitment/)
- August 2025: OP.GG removed its spell and skill tracker overlay because of Riot policy changes — [OP.GG Help Center](https://help.op.gg/hc/en-us/articles/48461098284953-The-spell-skill-tracker-overlay-is-no-longer-available)
- 29 Sep 2026 announcement: from 6 Oct 2026, "Riot will begin proactively preventing game memory access by default for all unknown third-party applications" in all titles (LoL, TFT, VALORANT). Official APIs are "the only allowed mechanism for retrieving game data". Known, approved tools have a grace period "until April 2027" to migrate, request new API data, or retire features. The reason given is that AI-assisted reverse engineering drove a surge of cheats and bot farms, and universal blocking is "the only viable way to protect game integrity at this scale". No formal allow list is published — [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/riot/performance/game-memory-access-removed-for-third-party-apps)
- Riot says the change "does not result in bans by itself". Scripts, bots and tools that expose hidden information remain bannable, up to permanent suspension — [Riot Support: third-party applications](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/riot/events/third-party-applications) (via [Hexgate](https://hexgate.app/blog/riot-third-party-apps/))
- Patch 26.20 (6 Oct 2026): replay access was limited to matches you played in, and third-party apps and sites lost replay downloads entirely — [LoL patch 26.20 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-20-notes/) (via [Hexgate](https://hexgate.app/blog/riot-third-party-apps/))
- As of 7 Oct 2026, Hexgate found no public statement on the memory change from Blitz, Porofessor, OP.GG, U.GG, Mobalytics or Overwolf — [Hexgate, vendor](https://hexgate.app/blog/riot-third-party-apps/)

### Inferences
- Live in-match information is the feature class publishers restrict first (timers, cooldowns, trackers). Pre-game builds and post-game match history survive. MLBB Guru's in-match "buy next" advice falls in the at-risk class, even though screenshots are less invasive than memory reading.
- Riot now names AI-driven cheating as a policy driver, so AI tools that read the screen in real time are likely to face more scrutiny from publishers, not less.
- Ad revenue is also a policy lever: Riot's overlay-ad ban hit the main revenue line of ad-funded companion apps. A business that relies on in-game overlay ads carries platform risk. A separate phone or web surface (MLBB Guru's design) avoids that specific lever.
- Moonton offers no official public data API comparable to Riot's (Coach Legend relies on a "public community" data service). An MLBB tool therefore depends on an unofficial data source that could disappear without notice.

### Gaps
- No primary Moonton or ByteDance statement on screen-reading or AI-advice tools, and no reported ban of an MLBB companion app.
- Riot's full Developer Portal policy text was not retrieved; only the support article was.
- No data on the revenue impact of Riot's 2025 ad ban for Blitz, Porofessor or Overwolf.

## 5. Free-to-paid conversion and pricing benchmarks for gaming companion subscriptions; ARPU (mobile)

### Takeaway
No gaming-companion company discloses conversion rates. The best general benchmark (RevenueCat) is a ~2% median download-to-paid rate for subscription apps, ~2.2% for freemium, ~12% for hard paywalls. Gaming-category conversion is about a third of health and fitness (~1%), and emerging markets are below 0.2%. For a product aimed at new MLBB players in Southeast Asia, that points to very low subscription conversion. It favors ads, rewarded ads and micro-transaction credits over subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat 2025 dataset: ~2.0% of downloads convert to paid within 35 days (global median, as reported by a secondary source) — [RocketShip HQ summary](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/revenuecat-state-of-subscription-apps-2025-summary/); [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- Hard paywall vs freemium: 12.11% vs 2.18% median download-to-paid. Other summaries give 10.7% vs 2.1% as Day-35 trial-to-paid, so the metric labels conflict between secondary sources — [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/revenuecat-state-of-subscription-apps-2025-summary/); [ARPU Brothers](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/2025-saas-mobile-apps-trends/)
- Regional: emerging markets such as Latin America and MEA report median conversion below 0.2%. In the 2026 edition, North America's Day-35 download-to-paid median is ~2.6% and the App Store median is 2.9% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [RevenueCat 2026 trends blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- Category: Health & Fitness ~2.9% download-to-paid (2026 edition). Gaming is reported at roughly a third of that (secondary summary, not verified on RevenueCat's page) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Companion-app price points: Blitz $3.99–4.99/month (regional), U.GG Plus $3.99/month or $29.88/year, Dota Plus $3.99/month, Porofessor ~$5/month (weak source). AI coach Trophi.ai is the outlier at $12.99–69.99/month — see section 1 sources and [Trophi pricing](https://www.trophi.ai/sim-racing/pricing/)
- Overwolf recommends freemium usage caps ("limited number of free uses per month") to drive upgrades, and annual plans at a 20–30% discount — [Overwolf subscriptions guide](https://dev.overwolf.com/ow-native/guides/growth/subscriptions/)
- Human-coaching marketplace benchmark: Metafy's monthly GMV and revenue grew from $185,975 to $375,971 (+102%), while personnel costs were ~$607k–746k/month, so it was still deeply unprofitable **[pre-2025, Sep 2022]** — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/19/video-game-coaching-marketplace-metafy-cuts-23-of-staff-despite-doubling-revenue/)

### Inferences
- An illustrative calculation, not sourced data: at 2% download-to-paid and $3.99/month, 100k installs would give ~2,000 subscribers, or ~$8k/month gross before app-store fees (15–30%). If SEA behaves like the cited emerging markets (<0.2%), the result is under ~$800/month. That may not cover Gemini vision inference for heavy in-match screenshot use, so per-screenshot cost needs to be modelled.
- Hard paywalls convert ~5x better per download. They suit a niche, high-intent offer (for example a paid "ranked climb" coaching pack) better than a mass-market free tool.
- A subscription is unlikely to be the main revenue line for a mobile MOBA coach in SEA. Credit packs and rewarded ads (Coach Legend), creator or esports partnerships, or B2B (selling to teams, tournament organizers, or publishers) are more likely models.

### Gaps
- No public ARPU, subscriber counts, or conversion rates for any MOBA companion app (Mobalytics, Blitz, U.GG, OP.GG, Porofessor), or for mobile game-companion apps specifically.
- No SEA-specific (Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia) subscription conversion benchmark was found. The <0.2% figure is for LatAm and MEA and is used as a proxy only.
- The exact RevenueCat gaming-category figure was not verified on the primary page.

## 6. Failures, pivots and exits among game-coaching startups

### Takeaway
Few game-coaching startups announced a shutdown. Most ended in small acquisitions by hardware or platform companies: Gamer Sensei to Corsair (2020), GOSU.AI to Sber (2021), GamersRdy to Metafy (2022), Mobalytics to ESL FACEIT Group (2025). Others went quiet. Human-coaching marketplaces struggled with unit economics (Metafy cut 23% of staff in 2022 despite doubling revenue). Some smaller AI-coaching startups (Kaikoo, GamerCoach) are listed as dead.

### Cited Findings
- Gamer Sensei, "the world's biggest esports coaching platform" (human coaching marketplace), was acquired by Corsair. PitchBook dates the deal to 9 Nov 2020, and it became a Corsair subsidiary brand alongside Elgato, ORIGIN PC and SCUF **[pre-2025]** — [TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/274417/take-your-game-to-the-next-level-corsair-acquires-gamer-sensei); [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/joao-silva/corsair-acquires-the-esports-coaching-platform-gamer-sensei/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/160968-25)
- GOSU.AI (an AI voice assistant and coach for LoL and CS) was acquired by Sber in 2021 after raising ~$1.9M + $2.8M **[pre-2025]** — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/gosu_ai); [Runa Capital](https://runacap.com/companies/gosu/); [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2019/12/10/gosu-ai-raises-2-8-million-ai-based-coaching-gamers-league-legends/)
- Metafy raised a $25M Series A (Feb 2022) and acquired rival coaching platform GamersRdy. In Sep 2022 it laid off 23% of staff (12 people, mostly product, design and engineering) even though GMV and revenue doubled. The founder said the company had prioritized GMV growth over unit economics, cut operational costs by 70%, and estimated just over two years of runway with no revenue. No 2024–2025 news was found **[pre-2025]** — [TechCrunch Series A](https://techcrunch.com/2022/02/01/metafy-lands-25m-to-scale-its-gaming-coaching-platform/); [TechCrunch layoffs](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/19/video-game-coaching-marketplace-metafy-cuts-23-of-staff-despite-doubling-revenue/); [TrueUp](https://www.trueup.io/co/metafy/layoffs)
- Kaikoo, an esports platform providing "connectivity, analytics, and automated coaching solutions", appears among 72 shut-down VC-backed gaming startups — [VCbacked Startup Graveyard](https://www.vcbacked.co/startup-graveyard/industry/gaming)
- GamerCoach (an online coaching app for gamers) is listed with status "Dead"; no closure date is given — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/gamers-room)
- Mobalytics was acquired by ESL FACEIT Group in March 2025 after ~$14–17M raised; terms not disclosed — [Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2025/03/esl-faceit-group-mobalytics-acquisition)
- Blitz is tied to TSM's parent Swift Media, which "helped acquire and run" the app, so it is effectively owned by an esports organization — [Google Ads publisher story](https://www.google.com/ads/publisher/stories/blitz/)

### Inferences
- The common pattern is that coaching tools reach millions of users but monetize thinly, and end up with a strategic buyer: hardware (Corsair), a bank or ecosystem player (Sber), or an esports or tournament group (EFG, TSM). For a solo or small MLBB product, a plausible exit is acquisition by an SEA esports org, tournament operator, MCN, or device/telco brand, rather than building a large standalone subscription business.
- Human-coaching marketplaces (Gamer Sensei, Metafy) had poor unit economics. AI lowers the cost of delivering coaching, but section 5 shows willingness to pay is the binding constraint, not delivery cost.

### Gaps
- No shutdown or pivot information was found for ProGuides, GameLeap, Insights.gg, iTero, or Dojo Madness, despite targeted searches. Their status is unknown, not confirmed as failed.
- Metafy's current status (2024–2026) and Gamer Sensei's status inside Corsair are unknown.
- No post-mortems from founders explaining shutdowns were found.
