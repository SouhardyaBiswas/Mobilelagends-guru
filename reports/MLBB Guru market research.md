# Give away drafts, charge for live coaching

**Verdict: yes, you can sell MLBB Guru, but sell only the live, spoken coach that adapts to the enemy team, because nothing else on the market does that and everything else you offer is free elsewhere. Before you charge anyone, rename it, remove the Moonton and Fandom art, run a paid Gemini key yourself behind an 18+ gate, and replace the iPad + Shortcut + bring-your-own-key setup with a phone-first flow. Price it at ₱99 / Rp29k (about US$1.7) a month or ₱249 / Rp79k per ranked season, and expect side income (low hundreds to low thousands of US dollars a month), not a venture business.** I found no MLBB tool that reads the live match or speaks to the player. The 17 companion apps I surveyed on Google Play and about ten fan sites all stop at draft counters, static builds and post-game screenshot review, and most tune their data for Epic-to-Mythic climbers rather than true beginners. Willingness to pay is thin: the average MLBB player spends about **US$1.58 a year** in the app stores, and the closest AI competitor sells 200 answers for US$0.99. Your costs only work if you slow screenshots from one every 15 seconds to about one a minute on a Flash-Lite model (about US$1 per user per month). On the app's current default, Gemini 3.8 Flash reading every 15 seconds, one paying user costs about **US$12.60 a month**, and that doubles in January 2027. The biggest blockers are contract terms and setup, not competitors. Gemini's terms bar apps "likely to be accessed" by under-18s, and the current setup screens out the confused beginners the product is built for. Spend the next 30 days and under US$100 proving demand with a pre-sale page, daily TikTok clips and 20 closely supported beta users before you write more code.

## Nobody else coaches during the match, and everything before it is free

The MLBB helper market is crowded and has no leader. The two biggest companion apps on Google Play, **BBML Build and MLBB Support, have about 100K+ installs each, against the game's 500M+** ([Google Play: BBML Build](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=bb.ml.build&hl=en&gl=US); [Google Play: MLBB](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobile.legends&hl=en&gl=PH)). Every one of the 17 apps surveyed gives away the same core: counter-picks, tier lists, builds, emblems and spells. The web looks the same. In October 2026, mlbb.gg drew **557K visits** and mlbb.io **352K**. mlbb.io was down from 1.16M in December 2025 ([Semrush: mlbb.gg](https://www.semrush.com/website/mlbb.gg/overview/); [Semrush: mlbb.io](https://www.semrush.com/website/mlbb.io/overview/)), and a search snippet showed it advertising itself for sale at **US$7,500** ([mlbb.io](https://mlbb.io/)). That is a fair price signal for draft data on its own. Moonton covers the basics too. Its official Hero Ranking tool lists counter heroes by rank bracket ([NeuralBoost](https://neuralboost.gg/blog/mobile-legends-hirara-guide-skills-combos-and-ban-rate)), and the in-game shop shows a recommended build and "flashes the next item you have to buy" ([Codashop guide](https://news.codashop.com/my/?p=19592)). A generic "buy this next" just repeats what the game already shows, so your advice has to change visibly with the enemy team: anti-heal against a healer, magic defense against a fed mage.

Every AI product so far works before or after the match. **Coach Legend** (10K+ installs) is a draft and chat coach paid in credits: 10 free, 10 more per rewarded ad, and **200 credits for US$0.99** on iOS ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coach-legend-mlbb-ai-guide/id6758374622)). **ML Coach** analyses a scoreboard screenshot you upload after the game ([mlcoach.online](https://mlcoach.online/)). BBML Build offers an "AI draft assistant" and an "in-game bubble" that shows static builds. Itembuild's Android overlay adapts a build to enemy picks you type in, and stresses that "the app never reads your screen" ([Google Play: Itembuild](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.itembuild.draft&hl=en&gl=US)). A third AI coach, Mythic Coach, appears to have been dropped: its package ID now opens a music app ([Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akrh.mlb_guide&hl=en_NZ)). None of them reads gold, the scoreboard or enemy items during the match, speaks, or re-plans when an enemy snowballs, and that is exactly what MLBB Guru does.

There is a real opening at the beginner level. MythicIQ draws its data "from Epic rank and above", mlbb.io's tier list uses "Mythic and above", and MOBA Wiki ranks heroes by Mythic win rates ([MythicIQ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mythiciq.app&hl=en); [mlbb.io](https://mlbb.io/en); [MOBA Wiki](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobawiki.mlbb&hl=en&gl=US)). High-rank win rates favour mechanically hard heroes that beginners can't play well. Your README says your engine uses all-rank win rates, so say so loudly. What beginners complain about also matches your feature list. There are too many heroes, and patches land every 3–6 weeks ([PixelTwelve](https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/mobile-legends-bang-bang-beginner-guide-roles-lanes-objectives-ranked-play)). The tutorial "barely covers several important aspects of gameplay" ([Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/5-tips-tricks-get-better-mobile-legends-bang-bang)). Players get stuck in Epic because "they don't counter-pick or adapt" ([ONE Esports](https://www.oneesports.gg/mobile-legends/how-to-rank-up-epic-mlbb/)). Ranks reset roughly every three months, so the need comes back every season ([PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/articles/086102/mobile-legends-ranks)). The coach can't fix the biggest category of complaints, which is toxic teammates and matchmaking ([Sikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/mobile-legends-us/i-keep-losing-in-mlbb-because-matchmaking-pairs-me-with-clueless-toxic-players)), so don't promise that it will.

The risk to this gap is that platform owners give the same thing away. In October 2025 Microsoft put Gaming Copilot on the Xbox mobile app as a second-screen assistant ([TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/341140/gaming-copilot-beta-is-coming-to-windows-pc-and-xbox-on-mobile)). Tencent built "Lingbao" into Honor of Kings. It gives live tactical alerts and reportedly buys items on voice command, and a report claims **10M+ players used it on day one**. It is free, and Tencent earns from it by selling cosmetic skins for it ([93913.com](https://www.93913.com/112107.html)). In July 2026 Moonton said it is building AI coaching tools, aimed at teams for now, with no timeline ([GamerHeadlines](https://gamerheadlines.com/moontons-ray-ng-on-mlbb-at-esports-world-cup-western-growth-and-ai-coaching/)). Riot's record shows that live in-match features are the first thing publishers restrict. Enemy ultimate timers were pulled from LoL apps in March 2025 ([porofessor.org](https://porofessor.org/guides/porofessor-vs-blitz/)), and from October 2026 Riot blocks game-memory access for all unknown apps by default ([Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/riot/performance/game-memory-access-removed-for-third-party-apps)). If Moonton ships its own Lingbao, your live loop loses most of its value overnight. Draft education, post-match review and explanations in the player's language would lose much less, so build the business so those parts can stand on their own.

## Today's setup reaches about 3% of players and uses up a free key in five minutes

Count the steps a newcomer has to get through today. They need an iPad to play on and a second phone. They have to sign up at Google AI Studio for a key, build a six-action Shortcut with a POST header, and start it every match. MLBB has **about 105–110M monthly players** ([Udonis, AppMagic data](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/mobile-legends-bang-bang)), but iOS is only **about 21% of tracked monthly users**: 12.1M on the App Store against 45.3M on Google Play ([Buffget](https://buffget.com/zh-CN/news/mobile-legends-bang-bang-player-count-november-2025-453m-monthly-stats-and-trends-sg5l0a)). No source measures how many play on an iPad. If 10–20% of iOS players do (my assumption), the reachable pool is **2–4M players, or 2–4% of the player base**, and that is before the key and Shortcut steps shrink it further. The people who finish that setup are power users. The confused beginners the product exists for, who don't yet know what anti-heal is, are the least likely to finish it. Your own README also lists the two weakest links as untested: whether a repeating Shortcut keeps running while the game is open, and whether voice works in iPhone and iPad Safari.

Bring-your-own-key has a worse problem than friction. The app defaults to `gemini-3.8-flash` (`src/vision/index.ts`), whose free tier reportedly allows only **20 requests a day** ([ScriptByAI](https://www.scriptbyai.com/gemini-api-free-tier-limits/); [Google AI forum](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/gemini-3-8-flash-free-tier-20-rpd-is-too-limited-for-practical-evaluation/180609)). At one screenshot every 15 seconds, that is **five minutes of a single match** before a new user's key hits its quota. The Flash-Lite models get about 500 free requests a day: enough for about 8 matches a day at one read every 15 seconds, or about 26 at one a minute. On the free tier, Google may use anything sent "to provide, improve, and develop" its products, and "human reviewers may read, annotate, and process your API input and output" ([Gemini API Terms](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms)). That is fine for your own testing, not for a product people pay for. Your code's fallback model, `gemini-2.5-flash`, is deprecated, and from 1 December 2026 its requests go to 3.5 Flash-Lite and are billed at that model's rates ([Gemini pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing)).

The fix is a phone-first flow where the user never sees a key. You already have the right free entry point: the phone-only Draft tab. Players tap in the enemy heroes and get counters, emblem, spells and a lane plan without a key. It runs on your own rules-based engine with no AI model, so each extra user costs you nothing. Make it the front door. For the paid live coach, host the key yourself and design for Android, which is about 79% of tracked monthly users. Let players send a screenshot to the web app from the Android share sheet when they die or open the shop (installed web apps can receive shares on Android; I haven't tested it for this flow). Keep the iPad Shortcut as a "pro setup" for enthusiasts. One idea is worth a ten-photo experiment: point the second phone's camera at the game screen and read the frames. That would remove the Shortcut and work with any game device. Hold every new flow to one bar: a first-time player hears their first spoken tip within three minutes of opening the site.

## One screenshot a minute keeps a ₱99 plan profitable

People do pay for MLBB help, but mostly for results rather than lessons. App-store spending on MLBB was **US$166M in 2025 across about 105M monthly players**, about US$1.58 per player per year ([Udonis, AppMagic data](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/mobile-legends-bang-bang)). A US$1.7 monthly plan therefore costs more each month than the average player spends on the game in a year, so you are selling to a small paying minority. That minority spends real money on *joki* (rank boosting). Epic stars sell for Rp7,000–10,000 and Legend stars for Rp10,000–15,000 ([Lapakgaming](https://www.lapakgaming.com/id-id/joki-mlbb); [VCGamers](https://www.vcgamers.com/news/harga-joki-legend-ke-mythic/)). At an assumed 25 stars per tier, clearing one tier costs **Rp175k–375k (US$10–22)**, even though Moonton bans boosted accounts ([Duniagames](https://duniagames.co.id/amp/discover/article/pro-kontra-jasa-joki-di-game-online/en)). Human coaches charge US$10–30 an hour on Metafy ([Metafy](https://metafy.gg/mobile-legends-bang-bang/sessions)). PC MOBA helper subscriptions cost US$3.99 a month, the price of both U.GG Plus and Valve's own Dota Plus ([Hawk.live](https://hawk.live/posts/dota-2-plus-guide); [Hexgate](https://hexgate.app/blog/blitz-vs-u-gg/)). SEA's best-known subscriptions sit at ₱169 for Spotify and ₱189 / Rp71,400 for YouTube Premium ([Spotify PH](https://www.spotify.com/ph-en/premium/); [PriceWorld](https://priceworld.com/indexes/youtube-premium/)). Pricing below all of those, with weekly and per-match "sachet" options, fits how SEA buys. The selling line almost writes itself: a whole season of coaching for less than the price of one boosted tier, with no ban risk.

## Four fixes stand between the prototype and a paid product

**The most concrete blocker is Google's own contract.** The Gemini API Additional Terms (effective 23 March 2026) say "You must be 18 years of age or older to use the APIs". They also forbid using the API in an app "directed towards or is likely to be accessed by individuals under the age of 18" ([Gemini API Terms](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms)). MLBB is rated **12+** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kh/app/id1160056295)), and its largest age group is 16 to 34 ([Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/mobile-legends-bang-bang)). A beginner coach for it is clearly likely to be used by teens, and if Google suspends the key, the product stops working. Bring-your-own-key doesn't obviously get you out, because your site is still the app calling the API (my reading; ask a lawyer). There are three ways through. First, gate accounts at 18+ with an age declaration, an 18+ ToS and marketing aimed at adults; that lowers the risk but doesn't remove it. Second, move the screenshot reading to a provider whose terms allow teen users; Vertex AI on Google Cloud runs under different terms, which I didn't check. Third, read the numbers on the device itself. That is more realistic than it sounds. Your engine already ignores item icons because vision models misread them, and builds its picture from printed gold, clock and KDA (README), which is the kind of text on-device text recognition handles well. Whichever you choose, paying users must run only on the paid tier. It is the only tier where Google "doesn't use your prompts or responses to improve our products", and the only one allowed for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland ([Gemini API Terms](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms)).

**The name and the pictures are the likeliest triggers for a takedown.** The US application for "MLBB" was abandoned in 2021, but "MOBILE LEGENDS: BANG BANG" and "MOONTON" are registered trademarks ([Justia: MLBB](https://trademark.justia.com/886/66/mlbb-88666755.html); [Justia: MLBB registration](https://trademarks.justia.com/886/66/mobile-legends-bang-88666723.html)). Moonton has been through two IP lawsuits with Riot ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/riot-sues-mobile-legends-bang-bang-maker-for-reasons-you-can-probably-guess)). Apple's rule 4.1(c) says "You cannot use another developer's icon, brand, or product name in your app's icon or name, without approval from the developer" ([Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). Google Play suspends apps that use other parties' trademarks in a way likely to confuse ([Google Play IP policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888072)). The art is a bigger problem. Your item pictures come from the Fandom wiki and your hero portraits from Moonton's API (README). Fandom's CC BY-SA licence covers text only, "not other media like images" ([Fandom Help: Licensing](https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Licensing)), and I found no Moonton fan-content or developer licence. Rename to a distinct brand with a descriptive tagline, such as "[Brand]: unofficial coach for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang players". In the paid product, replace portraits and icons with text labels or your own coloured tiles. Keep the non-affiliation line you already have, and publish a takedown contact. Using hero and item names to refer to the game carries much lower risk.

**Moonton's rules leave the live coach in a grey zone, so present it as a learning tool.** Moonton's ToS reportedly bans using "automated scripts, software, code or systems to collect information from or otherwise interact with the Services". Its penalty table lists "Install unauthorized software" at anything from a warning to a 30-day suspension ([NeuralBoost reproduction](https://neuralboost.gg/blog/mobile-legends-cheating-bans-what-moontons-notices-say)). I couldn't fetch the live text, so read and screenshot it yourself. Enforcement so far has targeted tools that reveal hidden information. In April 2026 Moonton and Central Java police shut down the Senpai Mod cheat network ([Moonton](https://en.moonton.com/news/322.html)), and Moonton has handed out 30-year bans for revealing enemy positions ([NeuralBoost](https://neuralboost.gg/blog/mobile-legends-cheating-bans-what-moontons-notices-say)). I found no action against a guide or coaching app. Riot is the publisher that has drawn a clear line, and it puts "buy this next" on the wrong side. Valorant's policy rejects real-time data "that would improve a player's performance immediately by altering player behavior (i.e. 'go here now')" ([Riot developer docs](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/valorant)). Overwolf bans "notifications that dictate player action based on the current game state" in apps for Riot games ([Overwolf](https://dev.overwolf.com/ow-native/guides/game-compliance/riot-games)). Official MLBB esports goes further and bans phones and tablets from the play area ([MSC 2026 rules](https://cdn.esportsworldcup.com/resources/uploads/MSC_at_EWC_26_Rulebook_c5937d8775.pdf)).

The defensible position follows from that. Show no hidden information, install nothing on the game device, automate no input and ask for no account login. Lead your marketing with draft prep and post-match review, call the live coach "learning mode", and never market it to tournament players. For true beginners this framing costs little, because guides tell them to learn new heroes in Classic or vs A.I. and to stay in Classic until they own a hero for each role ([Metammo](https://metammo.com/mobile-legends-guide/guides/beginners-guide)). It costs more with the ranked climbers stuck in Epic, who are the most likely to pay. That is the central tension of this business: its most profitable use is also its riskiest.

**The fourth fix is the setup itself, and slower risks come with it.** The setup fix is the phone-first, no-key flow described above. The slower risks start with child-data rules, which tighten through 2027. India's DPDP Rules require verifiable parental consent for under-18s, on a roughly 18-month rollout from November 2025 ([Legal500 / Saga Legal](https://www.legal500.com/firms/237811-saga-legal/c-india/news-and-developments/the-privacy-timer-starts-now-analysing-the-dpdp-rules-and-its-18-month-countdown)). Indonesia's GR 33/2026 applies from **16 January 2027** ([PwC Indonesia](https://www.pwc.com/id/en/pwc-publications/services-publications/digital-publications/government-regulation-33-2026-personal-data-protection.html)), and GR 17/2025 has a reported compliance deadline in March 2027 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/data-protection/1633198/gr-172025-indonesia-imposes-child-protection-duties-on-online-platforms)). The Philippine privacy regulator adopted an advisory on children's data aimed at games in September 2026 ([Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/43940-national-privacy-commission-adopted-advisory-on-processing-of-childrens-personal-data)). An 18+ product covers most of this if it also crops screenshots to the gold and scoreboard areas on the device, blurs names and chat, stores no images, names Gemini as a sub-processor in its privacy policy, and geo-blocks the EEA, UK and Switzerland. The legal research assumed you are based in India. If so, market to Indian players under the local title **Moba Legends: 5v5!** and never suggest a VPN or sideloading the original game, which India blocked in 2020 ([GamingOnPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/news/moba-legends-5v5-is-the-official-indian-version-of-mobile-legends-bang-bang)). Finally, the "public stats API" is the undocumented backend of Moonton's website and uses signed requests, and community wrappers describe themselves as "unofficial" ([GitHub: api-mobilelegends](https://github.com/ridwaanhall/api-mobilelegends)). Fetch from it at a low rate and cache the results, keep a fallback dataset, and never pass user requests straight through to it.

## Creators, TikTok and the M8 window beat paid ads

Your audience discovers content on TikTok and learns on YouTube. MLBB peaked at **2,538,922 concurrent viewers on TikTok Live** in January 2026 ([Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/tiktok-live-march-2026-mlbb-peaks)), and during M7 TikTok outdrew YouTube in Indonesia, Malaysia and the Philippines ([Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/m7-world-championship-hits-record-over-3-6-million-viewers)). The English-language hubs are smaller but easy to reach: the official Discord has **723,200 members** with teammate-finder channels by region and rank ([Discord](https://discord.com/servers/mobile-legends-bang-bang-342452785658331137)), and r/MobileLegendsGame had 120k members back in 2021 ([AFKGaming](https://afkgaming.com/mobileesports/guide/mobile-legends-giveaway-may-2021-how-to-enter)) and runs a pinned weekly discussion thread. Use English for your first 20 testers and localize for scale. Bahasa Indonesia comes first: Indonesia has the largest reported player base at about 35M monthly players ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/mobile-legends-bang-bang/mobile-legends-esports-sea-empire/)), and MPL ID Season 15 peaked at 4.13M viewers ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/general/news/most-watched-esports-events-2025)). Taglish voice comes next, then Malay. Your coach already generates its lines, so spoken advice in Bahasa or Taglish is cheap to add and is a better headline than "counter-pick stats", which every competitor already shows.

The product's output is its best advertising. "I let an AI coach my match" clips, "enemy locked Ling, here's your counter and build in 10 seconds" screen recordings, and "new counters after the patch" posts all come straight from normal use. Every answer can also become a vertical card with your URL watermarked on it. The cheapest launch moment is **M8**, running 17 December 2026 to 23 January 2027, with the main stage in Istanbul ([The Loadout](https://theloadout.com/m8-world-championship)). Post a "pro draft explained for beginners" clip within hours of each series, timed for Indonesian and Filipino viewers, and use no M8 or MLBB logos. Creators are affordable but worth paying only once you have proof a video converts. Philippine nano creators charge **₱1,000–5,000 per post** ([Spiralytics](https://www.spiralytics.com/blog/influencer-marketing-philippines)), Indonesian nano posts start around Rp200k ([Olakses](https://olakses.com/?p=30881)), and small gaming TikTokers list US$100–500 per short ([Collabstr: Happylio](https://collabstr.com/happylio); [Collabstr: Kein Moytod](https://collabstr.com/keinmoytod)). For a solo developer, the equivalent is one mid-size creator or guild adopting the coach as "theirs".

Trust is part of the pitch. ## Conclusion

The research changes what MLBB Guru is. It isn't a counter-pick tool with extras: counters are a commodity that every fan site pulls from the same Moonton endpoints. It is a translator that turns a screenshot into one sentence, in the player's own language, that a beginner can act on before the next fight. That sentence is the part worth paying for, and it is also the part a publisher can take over, as Tencent did with Lingbao. So the durable business is the learning loop around the live tip: post-match "why you fell behind", progress across a season, Bahasa and Taglish voice, and an audience built through creators. The live coach is the hook that draws people in. The realistic ceiling for a solo product is a few thousand dollars a month, or a small acquisition by an SEA esports organization, creator network or telco, the path Mobalytics, GOSU.AI and Gamer Sensei took.

The binding constraint isn't competition, demand or even cost. It is Google's under-18 clause, and it is worth solving before growth rather than after. If reading the printed numbers on the device proves accurate, it removes the age-clause exposure, the free-tier privacy problem and most of the per-user cost in one move, which would let you offer a much larger free tier without losing money. That one experiment is worth more than any marketing push, so run it in week one next to the pre-sale page.
