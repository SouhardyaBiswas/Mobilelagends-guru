# MLBB-Specific Competitive Landscape for "MLBB Guru" (as of 22 Nov 2026)

Method note: Google Play figures (installs, rating, review count, last-updated date, ads/IAP flags) were scraped directly from the US English Play listing pages (hl=en&gl=US) on 2026-11-22 unless marked "PH store". Play shows no star rating when an app has too few reviews; "no rating shown" means that. Traffic figures come from Semrush's public overview pages (October 2026 data, updated 11 Nov 2026). Similarweb's public pages returned no content (HTTP 202), and Reddit blocked automated access, so neither is used. Items dated before 2025 are flagged **[pre-2025, possibly outdated]**.

## 1. MLBB companion apps on Google Play / App Store

### Takeaway
There are many fan-made MLBB companion apps, and all of them are small. The largest has about 100K+ Play installs. The base game has 500M+. Almost every app has the same core: counter-pick lookup, tier list, builds, emblems and spells, all free and usually ad-supported. A newer group (2025–2026) adds AI chat on a credit model (Coach Legend), Android floating overlays used during draft and the match (BBML Build, Itembuild, Battle Buddy), and draft simulators against a bot (DraftMate, MLCounters). None of them reads the live match or speaks to the player.

### Inferences
- The app market is crowded but has no leader. No companion app passes about 100K installs, about 0.02% of the game's 500M+ Play installs. Distribution and trust are still open.
- Counter-pick plus build plus emblem and spell advice is a commodity, usually free. It cannot be MLBB Guru's main selling point. Even "why this pick" explanations already exist (MythicIQ, Coach Legend, Itembuild, DraftMate).
- Overlays are possible only on Android, and the overlay apps are careful to say they are read-only and do not read the screen (Itembuild). That suggests developers see screen-reading as a ToS risk. MLBB Guru's iPad-to-Shortcut-to-phone setup avoids an overlay but still uses screenshot reading.
- Many apps offer Bahasa Indonesia (Itembuild, DraftMate) or several languages (BBML: 11), which shows how much of the audience is in SEA.

- iOS App Store figures (ratings, rank) for most of these apps could not be collected. Only Coach Legend's iOS page was fetched.
- Sensor Tower, AppMagic and AppBrain download estimates for individual companion apps were not found or were paywalled.
- Play IAP price ranges for MLBB Support were not reliably attributable to the app, so they are left out.

## 2. MLBB stats and guide websites and their traffic

### Takeaway
There are about ten active fan stats and draft sites, mostly free, ad- or donation-supported, and refreshed daily or every 2 hours. Moonton's own site, mobilelegends.com (which includes an official Hero Ranking tool), is far bigger, at about 7.7M visits a month on Semrush's estimate. The top fan sites, mlbb.gg and mlbb.io, get roughly 0.35–0.56M a month. mlbb.io's traffic fell from about 1.16M (Dec 2025) to 352K (Oct 2026).

**Official Moonton web data**
- mobilelegends.com has an official **Hero Ranking tool at mobilelegends.com/rank**. It shows PICK RATE, WIN RATE, BAN RATE and a COUNTER HERO column, filtered by rank bracket (for example "All") and time window (for example past 1 day or past 7 days). Official hero pages have Skills, Counters, Guides and Wallpaper tabs. The Counters tab has written counter advice plus a "Counter Score" and "Teammate Score" for five heroes each. The Skills tab has "Skill Combo" guidance, such as Jungler Combos and Teamfight Combos. — [NeuralBoost Hirara guide (reads Moonton pages, Oct–Nov 2026)](https://neuralboost.gg/blog/mobile-legends-hirara-guide-skills-combos-and-ban-rate)

### Inferences
- Most fan tools appear to scrape Moonton's own mobilelegends.com/rank data, directly or through the ridwaanhall API. That is the same data source MLBB Guru uses, so its data gives no edge. The edge has to come from how the data is used.
- Fan sites get modest traffic, about 0.3–0.6M visits a month each. Traffic is concentrated in Indonesia, the Philippines and Singapore, and it swings a lot from month to month (mlbb.io −70% from its Dec 2025 peak). Search and LLM referrals are small: mlbb.io received 229 visits from ChatGPT over 6 months, per Semrush.
- Draft simulators "vs AI" and live draft assistants are now standard on the web, so MLBB Guru's draft mode has no novelty value on its own.

- Similarweb estimates could not be retrieved.
- MLBB-specific traffic share of Liquipedia, ONE Esports and GosuGamers is unknown.

## 3. What Moonton provides itself (the biggest competitor)

### Takeaway
Moonton gives beginners a good set of static help: tutorial and beginner tasks, Training and vs-A.I. modes, in-game **Pro Builds** and custom "My Builds", a shop that suggests your next build item, and official counter and teammate scores and skill-combo tips on its hero pages and Hero Ranking tool. I found no evidence of an in-game AI coach, live draft advisor or voice guidance as of Nov 2026. Moonton has publicly said (July 2026) that it is **building AI coaching tools**, framed as match analysis for "certain teams" and esports. That is the clearest strategic threat.

### Cited Findings

**In-game help (current)**
- Pro Builds are in the Preparation tab, and players can save custom sets under "My Builds". — [ONE Esports beginners guide](https://www.oneesports.gg/mobile-legends/mobile-legends-beginners-guide/)
- "The recommended build in the shop is fine for your first weeks." The same guide tells beginners to switch to counter items (anti-heal, magic defense) once they understand why. That shows the default recommendation does not adapt to the enemy. It also says to "Finish the tutorial and the beginner tasks", use Classic or vs A.I. for a new hero's first ten matches, and play Classic until you own a hero for each position. Guide updated 30 Oct 2026 for Season 42, patch 2.2.16a. — [Metammo beginner guide](https://metammo.com/mobile-legends-guide/guides/beginners-guide)
- After you sell boots, "the shop will automatically recommend the first item on your list". So shop suggestions follow your preset build order. — [MLBB Central](https://mlbbcentral.com/mlbb-buy-and-sell-items-easy-way/)
- The official store listing describes "tap-to-equip" buying from anywhere on the map. — [App Store MLBB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1160056295)
- Guides refer to built-in tutorials, practice modes, AI matches and Training Mode. — [MLBBHub beginner guide 2026](https://mlbbhub.com/news/mlbb-beginner-guide-2026); [fastlordtopup beginners guide 2026](https://fastlordtopup.com/blog/mlbb-beginners-guide-2026-everything-new-players-need-to-know-to-win-more-matches)

**Official web stats and advice**
- Official Hero Ranking tool (mobilelegends.com/rank) shows win, pick and ban rates and a Counter Hero column by rank bracket and time window. Official hero pages show written counter advice, Counter and Teammate Scores, and skill combos. — [NeuralBoost (cites Moonton pages)](https://neuralboost.gg/blog/mobile-legends-hirara-guide-skills-combos-and-ban-rate)
- mlbb.gg imports "Moonton ranked matchups" and "official matchup advice". — [mlbb.gg](https://mlbb.gg/counter)

**Moonton and AI**
- Esports Insider (27 July 2026) interviewed Moonton's Ray Ng. He called AI a very important part of strategy, used daily for game development and esports. He said Moonton is developing its own AI tools to analyse games and give a coaching perspective to certain teams. No release timeline was given. — [Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2026/07/mlbb-moonton-ray-ng-esports-world-cup-interview) (the page returned 403 to fetching; content comes from the search summary); [GamerHeadlines recap, 30 Jul 2026](https://gamerheadlines.com/moontons-ray-ng-on-mlbb-at-esports-world-cup-western-growth-and-ai-coaching/)
- GamerHeadlines paraphrases the tools as "AI-powered coaching tools designed to help players improve" by analysing match data and giving personalized recommendations, with no timeline. TrendHunter frames them as automated match analysis for competitive teams, aiming for broader availability later. — [GamerHeadlines](https://gamerheadlines.com/moontons-ray-ng-on-mlbb-at-esports-world-cup-western-growth-and-ai-coaching/); [TrendHunter](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/ai-coaching-tools)
- AI backlash: fans objected to an allegedly AI-generated main menu (Layla) on the MLBB Advanced Server. Patch 2.2.03.2 (20 July 2026) removed the alleged AI-generated 3D model, and Moonton did not confirm it was made with AI. — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/mobile-legends-bang-bang/mlbb-ai-generated-main-menu-layla-backlash/)
- Moonton reportedly set a DAU record on 18 March 2026, and the MSC at EWC 2026 peaked at 2.88M concurrent viewers. — [Wikipedia: Mobile Legends: Bang Bang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_Legends:_Bang_Bang)

**Third-party app policy**
- A secondary explainer says Moonton takes a firm line against unauthorized third-party apps, can detect them and may ban accounts permanently. It cites no ToS clause. — [NEMA repost explainer](https://erp.nema.gov.mn/today-chronicle/mobile-legends-and-third-party-apps-what-you-need-to-know-1764814841)

### Inferences
- Moonton already covers "what to build" for beginners, through Pro Builds and shop suggestions, and "who counters whom" through official counter scores. MLBB Guru's value over Moonton is advice that adapts to the live match: counter items against actual enemy items or fed enemies, gold-aware "buy next", and voice.
- The July 2026 AI coaching statement is aimed at esports teams for now and looks like post-match analysis. If Moonton brings in-client AI coaching to regular players, it would make third-party coaches unnecessary. Moonton controls the game data and needs no screenshots. Treat this as a medium-term platform risk.
- Fan reaction against AI-generated content in MLBB suggests that "AI" branding can draw some backlash in this community. Note this is about art assets, not coaching.

### Gaps
- I could not find official Moonton patch notes (2025–2026) that confirm or rule out draft-phase hero recommendations, lane pre-selection, or enemy-adaptive item suggestions in the client.
- I did not find Moonton's actual ToS or a list of allowed and banned third-party tools. Whether a screenshot-reading second-screen coach is allowed is unconfirmed and needs a legal and ToS review.
- I found nothing about an official Moonton companion app (aside from the separate idle RPG "Mobile Legends: Adventure", which is not a companion) or about details of an "MLBB Academy" (the community API lists an "academy" service group).

## 4. AI and real-time coaching tools for MLBB

### Takeaway
Several small "AI coach" products exist. All of them are pre-match (draft chat) or post-match (scoreboard screenshot analysis). I found **no tool that reads the live match screen, tracks gold or enemy items, or speaks coaching out loud**. The closest to in-match help are static Android overlays (BBML "in-game bubble", Itembuild, Battle Buddy) and manually entered counter-item suggestions (Itembuild, the former Mythic Coach "Build Doctor"). One AI coach app (Mythic Coach) appears to have been abandoned and its Play listing reused. I found no confirmed case of Moonton banning a coaching tool.

### Cited Findings
- **Coach Legend: MLBB AI Guide** (Kanto Projects; iOS developer listed as Jeff Bercasio): AI draft coach. Enter enemy picks to get the top 10 counters "ranked by how many enemy heroes they beat, their current win rate and how well they fit your team". One tap gives an AI breakdown. Also team synergy score out of 100, Hero Finder, and Coach Chat ("How do I lane against Ling?"). Advice is personalized by rank "(Warrior to Mythical Glory)", playstyle and roles. Credits: 10 free, +10 per rewarded ad (up to 10 a day), refunded on failure, Google or Apple sign-in required. iOS IAP "200 AI Credits $0.99". Play 10K+ installs, updated Sep 27, 2026. iOS: "hasn't received enough ratings", version 1.3 (Sep 27). Runs on iPhone, iPad and Android. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kantoprojects.draftlegend); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coach-legend-mlbb-ai-guide/id6758374622); [coachlegend.kantoprojects.com](https://coachlegend.kantoprojects.com/)
- **Mythic Coach: MLBB AI Guide**: indexed as a chat assistant whose "AI Coach answers in real-time", plus a "Build Doctor" that "automatically craft[s] counter-item builds for the enemy composition". The package (com.akrh.mlb_guide) now serves "Kinetic: Pendulum Music", which suggests the MLBB app was dropped. — [Search-indexed listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akrh.mlb_guide&hl=en_NZ)

### Inferences
- MLBB Guru's real-time loop (screenshot, vision model, gold, scoreboard and enemy items, spoken "buy X next", re-plan when an enemy is fed) has **no direct MLBB competitor** that I could find. Its nearest neighbours are static overlays and post-game screenshot analysis (ML Coach).
- Credit-metered LLM chat (Coach Legend: $0.99 for 200 credits plus rewarded ads) sets the current price expectation for AI MLBB help, which is very low.
- Competitors stress "never reads your screen". That suggests MLBB Guru's screenshot reading could become a ToS or perception issue if Moonton tightens rules. Using a separate device (iPad screenshot to phone) is less intrusive than an overlay, but it still automates reading match state.

### Gaps
- I found no evidence either way of an MLBB coaching tool being shut down or banned by Moonton. Mythic Coach's disappearance has no stated reason.
- Install and user numbers for mlcoach.online and the Blink prototype are unknown.
- Reddit, Discord and Telegram communities (for example mlbb.io's Telegram) could not be searched (Reddit blocked automated access), so unlisted Discord bots or GPTs may exist.

## 6. Gaps no current tool fills well for beginners

### Takeaway
Every MLBB tool, including Moonton's own, stops helping once the match loads, or offers only static builds in an overlay. Most data and marketing target Epic-to-Mythic climbers rather than true beginners. Nobody offers enemy-adaptive "what to buy next" based on live gold and enemy items, spoken guidance, a second-screen dashboard, or re-planning when an enemy snowballs. These are MLBB Guru's distinctive features. The risks are Moonton's ToS and Moonton's own AI coaching plans.

### Cited Findings
- High-rank focus: MythicIQ's data is "from Epic rank and above" and pitched at "Stuck in Epic or Legend?". mlbb.io's tier list uses "high-level ranks (Mythic and above)" and markets to "10,000+ Mythic players". MOBA Wiki's tiers use "real Mythic rank win rate data". DraftMate's tier lists start at Legend. — [MythicIQ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mythiciq.app&hl=en); [mlbb.io](https://mlbb.io/en); [MOBA Wiki](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mobawiki.mlbb&hl=en&gl=US); [DraftMate](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nexaimstudio.draftmate&hl=en&gl=US)
- Exception: Coach Legend personalizes by rank from Warrior upward, but only in pre-match chat. — [Coach Legend Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kantoprojects.draftlegend)
- In-match help is limited to static overlays (BBML in-game bubble, Battle Buddy, Itembuild), which need manual input and run only on Android. — [BBML](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=bb.ml.build&hl=en&gl=US); [Itembuild](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.itembuild.draft&hl=en&gl=US); [Battle Buddy](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.divy.mlbbguideoffline&hl=en&gl=US)
- Moonton's shop suggestion follows a preset list, and beginners are told to work out counter items on their own. — [Metammo](https://metammo.com/mobile-legends-guide/guides/beginners-guide); [MLBB Central](https://mlbbcentral.com/mlbb-buy-and-sell-items-easy-way/)
- Post-game AI exists (ML Coach, from scoreboard screenshots), but not during the match. — [mlcoach.online](https://mlcoach.online/)
- Draft help already includes explanations ("Why this pick?") and synergy scores in several free tools. — [MythicIQ](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mythiciq.app&hl=en); [DraftMate](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nexaimstudio.draftmate&hl=en&gl=US); [Coach Legend](https://coachlegend.kantoprojects.com/)

- **Biggest gap MLBB Guru fills:** live, enemy-aware itemization and macro reminders in plain spoken language for players who don't yet know what anti-heal or magic defense is for. No product does this today.
- **The beginner data problem:** most tools rank counters using Epic+ or Mythic data, which can mislead true beginners. Mechanically hard heroes can top high-rank win rates. A beginner-tuned filter (easy heroes, forgiving picks, all-rank or low-rank data) is an open angle.
- **Platform gap:** overlays are Android-only, and iOS players get no in-match companion. MLBB Guru's iPad plus phone second-screen design is unusual. It does depend on a two-device setup and an iOS Shortcut, which raises onboarding friction for casual new players.
- **Commoditized features:** counter picks, tier lists, builds, synergy scores, draft simulators and "why" explanations are free everywhere. The manual "tap in enemy heroes" draft mode won't stand out on its own.
- **Main threats:** (1) Moonton shipping official in-client AI coaching (announced for teams in July 2026). (2) Moonton ToS enforcement against automated screen reading. (3) Low willingness to pay, given $0.99 AI credit packs and $10 human sessions.

- I found no evidence on how reliable vision-model screenshot reading of the MLBB scoreboard or shop is, or how other tools have fared with it.
- Moonton's official third-party-tool policy text was not retrieved. This is critical before launching a screenshot-reading coach.
